Our Story & Philosophy

Where Culinary Heritage Meets Modern Alchemy

Founded in 2018, Aurelia was born from a simple obsession: to strip away the unnecessary and celebrate ingredients in their purest, most expressive form.

Crafted with patience in the heart of the city.

A Dialogue Between Fire, Earth, and Time

What began as a twelve-seat supper club has evolved into an intimate sanctuary for the curious palate. Our kitchen operates without freezers or shortcuts—only honest produce harvested at peak ripeness, aged in-house, and transformed over live fire.

Each plate tells a story of the hands that nurtured it, from the artisan ceramicists who shape our tableware to the independent winemakers whose biodynamic bottles line our cellar.

“We don’t cook to impress; we cook to evoke a memory you haven’t had yet.”

Julian Vance Executive Chef & Founder
