About Our Georgian Restaurant Chain in Dnipro

We bring the warmth of Caucasian hospitality, centuries-old culinary traditions, and fresh farm-to-table ingredients to the heart of Dnipro.

Our Story

Founded in Dnipro with a deep passion for Georgian culture, our chain began with a simple mission: to share the true spirit of a traditional Georgian supra (feast) with every guest. From our cozy locations on Soborna Square and Dmytra Yavornytskoho Avenue to Sichovykh Striltsiv Street, each restaurant is designed as a welcoming haven where friends and families gather.

Every morning, our bakers fire up the traditional clay tone oven to bake golden shoti bread, while our chefs hand-fold juicy khinkali and prepare freshly baked Adjarian khachapuri using authentic spices brought directly from Georgia.

🔥

Traditional Recipes

We stay true to time-honored Georgian cooking techniques, using real clay qvevri vessels, wood-fired ovens, and authentic Svanetian salt.

🌿

Fresh Local Ingredients

We partner with trusted local farmers in the Dnipropetrovsk region to source crisp herbs, ripe tomatoes, tender meats, and artisanal sulguni cheese daily.

🍷

Georgian Hospitality

In Georgia, a guest is considered a gift from God. Whether you dine in with us or order online, we treat every visitor like family.
